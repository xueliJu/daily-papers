### 1. **[A Privileged Teacher That Only Scores Cannot Search: Critique-Conditioned On-Policy Self-Distillation for Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13744v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 88/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-09
- **作者**: Mayug Maniparambil, Ayush Rai, Ekansh Verma et al.
- **标签**: `cs.AI` `cs.CL` `cs.LG`
- **AI摘要**: 提出批判条件化自蒸馏以提升推理能力
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

On-policy self-distillation promises to replace the sparse outcome signal of verifiable-reward RL with dense token-level credit: a teacher conditioned on the reference solution scores the student's own rollout, and the resulting distributional shift is distilled back into the policy. We investigate whether this scoring signal actually contains corrective credit. Using gold error spans in student rollouts, we test the two signals consumed by self-distillation objectives, the full-distribution KL and the token log-ratio, for two properties: whether they localize errors and whether they push probability away from erroneous student tokens. They exhibit neither of these properties nor can they reliably distinguish correct from incorrect rollouts within the same problem. We argue that this failure is structural: locating an error in a reasoning trace is itself a search problem, while a scoring teacher is never explicitly asked to perform that search. The same model, with the same privileged information, localizes errors substantially better when allowed to generate a critique. This forms the motivation for \emph{critique-conditioned self-distillation}: first generate a critique of the current rollout, then condition the scoring teacher on that critique. The resulting signal localizes errors, is corrective within error spans, and shifts probability toward tokens that initiate backtracking such as ``Wait'', ``But'', and ``Actually''. We further show that the critique is useful both at test time, where resuming a wrong rollout with the critique in context converts math problems the model cannot solve by sampling, and in cold-start GRPO, where it provides an advantage signal on prompts for which verifiable rewards alone provide none.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 揭示了传统评分蒸馏的结构性缺陷，并提出了有效的批判引导机制。

<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

An ideal (language) model must continually update its knowledge. Existing work largely focuses on making these updates efficient after training. ### 3. **[Same Query, Different Answer: Run-to-Run Variance in Semantic Query Processing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13823v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 82/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-09
- **作者**: Oktie Hassanzadeh, Dharmashankar Subramanian
- **标签**: `cs.DB` `cs.AI`
- **AI摘要**: 揭示语义查询中LLM运行结果的不稳定性及评估偏差
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Semantic query engines ask a large language model (LLM) to judge individual rows, and benchmarks compare these engines on the quality of a few executions at temperature 0. We measure how much another execution changes. We re-execute the text and tabular workloads of SemBench, LRO-Bench, and UDA-Bench through their own harnesses, log every one of 1.2 million model calls, and reanalyze SemBench's published executions. At temperature 0, between 0.07% and 10.2% of rows change their operator output over five executions, depending on the model, and up to 24.0% on one task. Plans amplify these changes exponentially in the number of rows an answer exposes, as a possible-worlds model predicts: on UDA-Bench, a few percent of extracted values change, but about two thirds of the answers do. In SemBench's published results, 21% of text results change quality between executions, including on engines whose plans are deterministic. Several per-query system comparisons, and 16 of 28 tests of LRO-Bench's implementation recommendations, are not settled by the executions the benchmarks run. Changing rows are rows on which models disagree with each other; identical requests within one execution disagree as often as across executions; and one execution's decision margin or reasoning length predicts which rows change. Voting reduces the variance but does not remove it, and a calibrated cascade keeps its guarantee while the rows it returns change. We derive reporting rules for benchmarks and design rules for engines.
</details>

### 4. **[ReplaySSM: Cache SSM Inputs, Not State](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13778v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 82/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-09
- **作者**: Ze-Wei Liou, Harris Nover, Amey Naik et al.
- **标签**: `cs.LG` `cs.AI`
- **AI摘要**: 通过缓存输入而非状态优化SSM推理效率
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

State-space models (SSMs) maintain a fixed-size recurrent state, but reading and writing it at every decoding step can bottleneck hybrid models. This eager state management causes large state I/O with low arithmetic intensity, leaves speculative decoding no cheap rollback, and feeds state quantization error into subsequent recurrent updates. We introduce ReplaySSM, which unrolls the recurrence to represent the state as a checkpoint and a buffer of recent SSM inputs. This separates output computation from checkpoint updates. By updating the checkpoint periodically, ReplaySSM avoids state writes on most steps and roughly halves the dominant state memory traffic. Keeping recent inputs also makes speculative rollback a buffer-pointer operation and enables output-only verification without materializing intermediate states. The same separation allows a low-precision checkpoint to serve output computation on most steps while a higher-precision checkpoint serves recurrent updates, so rounding error in the low-precision copy never enters later updates. We implement ReplaySSM in vLLM for Mamba-2 and Gated DeltaNet. At serving batch size 256, it accelerates standard decoding by $1.20$--$1.47\times$ over a tuned vLLM baseline across four hybrid models, and delivers about $1.8\times$ the throughput of vLLM's speculative decoding on the two 120B-class models. Separate state precisions also reduce output KL divergence and downstream accuracy losses compared with a shared low-precision checkpoint.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 提出创新缓存机制显著提升SSM推理速度与精度

### 5. **[DualQuant: Dual-scale Calibration-free Weight-and-Activation Quantization for LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13767v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 82/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-09
- **作者**: Mahsa Salmani, Sayeh Sharify, Hesham Mostafa
- **标签**: `cs.LG` `cs.AI`
- **AI摘要**: 提出无需校准的双尺度量化框架，提升LLM推理效率。
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Low-bit inference on accelerators with native 4-bit support requires quantizing both weights and activations to realize the full throughput gains these formats provide. While post-training weight quantization has been extensively studied, activation quantization remains considerably more challenging and typically requires calibration data. In this work, we introduce DUALQUANT, a dual-scale, calibration-free post-training quantization framework that directly minimizes the weight-reconstruction Mean Squared Error (MSE) and, as an immediate by-product, yields per-channel activation rescalings prior to activation quantization. Our key observation is that the scaling factors obtained by minimizing weight-reconstruction error provide effective activation rescalings, enabling competitive activation quantization without calibration data. We validate these gains with a real packed W4A4 inference path for Llama-3.1-8B on an RTX 4090. The resulting implementation closely matches the accuracy of fake quantization while delivering up to 2.61x memory reduction and up to 2.46x throughput speedup over BF16.
</details>
- **评分理由**: 方法创新且实用，实验验证充分，在量化领域表现优异。

### 1. **[Error Rate is Not Enough: Costs of Augmentation in Low-Resource ASR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13807v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 82/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-09
- **作者**: Katsumi Ibaraki, David Chiang
- **标签**: `cs.CL`
- **AI摘要**: 分析低资源ASR中数据增强的误差机制与互补性
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Data augmentation is standard practice for low-resource automatic speech recognition (ASR), but its benefits are almost always reported as aggregate error rates, leaving open why and when a method helps. On four low-resource languages and English at five data sizes, we cross two augmentations that modify existing audio (speed perturbation and SpecAugment) with two that modify text transcriptions and synthesize audio from it. We analyze errors at the phoneme level and find that they help for complementary reasons. SpecAugment has the most reliable aggregate improvements, but increases tone and palatalization errors, whereas text-based methods introduce consonant insertions; in combination, each reduces the errors the other introduces. This complementarity is predictable. Tone and palatalization are each signaled by a single acoustic property and occur least frequently in training (lowest training exposure), and text-based methods that flatten word frequency raise their frequency by 37--38%, reversing those errors while further improving aggregate error. The improvement from combining the methods exceeds the sum of their individual improvements; the superadditivity vanishes by 100 hours, limiting the complementarity to the low-resource regime. Where specific error types matter, we recommend selecting augmentation by training exposure and acoustic properties, both measurable before any training.
</details>

- **评分**: ⭐ 82/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-09
- **作者**: Adarsh Jamadandi, Nicolas Keriven
- **标签**: `cs.LG` `cs.AI`
- **AI摘要**: 揭示离散图扩散模型存在条件记忆化现象
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Discrete graph diffusion models (GDMs) corrupt the graph structure by adding noise independently to nodes and edges under a Markovian noise model. In the unconditional setting without node features, an equivariant denoiser trained on a single graph is able to generate novel graphs and sample-level metrics such as edge overlap and WL kernel similarity find no evidence of memorization. In this work, we challenge this view and show for the first time that graph diffusion models indeed exhibit memorization. Analyzing the reverse diffusion trajectories of GDMs trained on two-block stochastic block models (SBMs), we find a strong train-data bias: the model recovers a corrupted training graph almost perfectly compared to a held-out test graph. We term this conditional memorization, as it surfaces only when the reverse trajectory is conditioned on a corrupted training graph and is not apparent during inference. It coincides with a growing generalization gap, where the training loss improves as the loss on held-out graphs degrades, and it cannot be mitigated by early stopping. The onset of memorization always precedes the emergence of community structure in the generated graphs, thus every checkpoint earlier produces structureless graphs. This behavior holds across all node sizes we test.

### 4. **[Partially Informed Stochastic Optimization via Zeroth-Order Residual Tracking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13871v1)**
- **评分**: ⭐ 82/100 | **日期**: 2026-10-09
- **作者**: El Mahdi Chayti, Alireza Mirrokni, Martin Jaggi
- **标签**: `cs.LG`
- **AI摘要**: 提出PISO算法优化部分梯度信息问题
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Many optimization problems provide direct access to only part of the gradient, while the remaining component must be inferred from function evaluations. This structure arises in performative prediction, performative reinforcement learning, and bilevel optimization with a black-box lower level. We introduce Partially Informed Stochastic Optimization (PISO), which combines the available gradient component with a running estimate of the residual. By centring each zeroth-order observation on this estimate, PISO makes the variance due to random probing depend on the tracking error rather than the residual's magnitude. This yields a randomized sketch iteration with an explicit contraction mechanism and a principled interpretation of momentum. For smooth nonconvex objectives, we establish convergence guarantees that depend on the residual's variation and initial magnitude, without requiring a uniform bound on the unavailable gradient component. With independently sampled function evaluations and Gaussian or spherical probes, PISO attains leading function-evaluation complexities of \(O(d^{2}\varepsilon^{-6})\) under Lipschitz gradients and \(O(d^{2}\varepsilon^{-5})\) under an additional Lipschitz-Hessian assumption. These rates require only one probe direction, two function samples, and one known-component sample per update. The analysis also quantifies how batching trades observations for fewer deployments. Across five settings, PISO variants improve on the tested baselines with comparable oracle access in three, reach a comparable solution faster in bilevel routing, and perform best among methods without response-model derivatives in performative reinforcement learning.

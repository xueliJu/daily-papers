### 2. **[When KL Regularization Misfires in Group Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12161v1)**

- **AI摘要**: 揭示KL正则化在群策略优化中的失效模式并提出ZCPO方法
<details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Why does removing reference-policy KL regularization sometimes improve group policy optimization? This motivates studying how reference-policy information should enter group-relative updates. <details>
<summary>原始摘要</summary>

Unauthorized model distillation, in which a model is trained on the outputs of a proprietary large language model (LLM), is a growing threat to model providers. We study distillation inference: determining whether a suspect model was distilled from another model or trained independently. <summary>原始摘要</summary>
